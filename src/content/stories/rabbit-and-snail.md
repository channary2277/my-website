Long ago, beside a quiet river, there lived a rabbit. He was very fast, and he was very proud.

One day he saw a little snail on a leaf. "You are so slow!" laughed the rabbit. "It takes you all day to cross one rice field."

The snail looked up. "Maybe I am slow," she said. "But I can still beat you in a race."

The rabbit laughed so hard that he fell over. "A race? Fine! We will race along the river, to the big tree. Tomorrow morning."

That night, the snail called all her snail friends. They looked the same, small and brown. "Hide along the path by the river," she said. "When the rabbit calls, the snail in front of him must answer."

In the morning, the race began. The rabbit ran fast. After a while, he stopped and called, "Snail, where are you?"

"I'm here!" said a small voice in front of him.

The rabbit was surprised. He ran faster. Again he called, "Snail, where are you?"

"I'm here!" said a small voice, again in front of him.

This happened again and again. The rabbit ran until he was very tired. When he reached the big tree, a little snail was already sitting there, smiling.

The rabbit never laughed at the snail again.
