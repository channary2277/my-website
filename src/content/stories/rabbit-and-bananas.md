One hot morning, a rabbit was very hungry. He sat by the road and thought, "What can I eat today?"

Then he saw a woman walking to the market. On her head was a big basket, full of sweet, yellow bananas.

The rabbit had an idea. He ran ahead and lay down in the middle of the road. He closed his eyes and did not move at all.

The woman came near and saw him. "Oh! A rabbit, and it is dead," she said. "I will take it home for dinner."

She picked up the rabbit and put him in the basket, on top of the bananas. Then she kept walking.

Inside the basket, the rabbit opened one eye. He smiled. Quietly, he ate one banana. Then another. Then another.

When his belly was full, the rabbit jumped out of the basket and ran into the forest.

"Thank you for breakfast!" he called.

The woman looked in her basket. The bananas were gone, and so was her "dead" rabbit.
